# Hajime Japan Service Catalog

## How to use this catalog

This catalog defines Hajime's service units. Packages combine these services into a simple client offer; the catalog prevents hidden work and makes ownership clear internally.

Durations are planning ranges, not guarantees. They assume complete client inputs and available partners. Government, bank, landlord, insurer, and professional review times remain outside Hajime's control.

Estimated effort describes Hajime coordination effort:

- **Light**: up to several focused hours across the service window.
- **Standard**: recurring coordination across multiple days and parties.
- **High**: complex sequencing, repeated review, or several partner handoffs.
- **Custom**: cannot be responsibly estimated until scoped.

Partner professional fees and third-party costs are separate unless the package explicitly includes them.

## 1. Launch Strategy and Readiness Planning

**Purpose**: Turn a founder's Japan objective into a credible launch route, responsibility map, and decision-ready plan.

**Description**: Hajime gathers business, ownership, timing, location, capital, language, operating, and immigration-related context. The coordinator identifies open decisions, likely service needs, specialist questions, assumptions, and the proposed sequence. Qualified partners answer legal, tax, accounting, and immigration questions.

**Inputs**: Founder goals; business model; proposed activities; ownership and leadership; target city; timing; capital range; local presence; relocation or visa dependencies; known customers, partners, or hires.

**Outputs**: Launch brief; scope assumptions; proposed entity and professional decision questions; phase plan; responsibility map; preliminary timeline range; risk and dependency register; recommended package and partner capabilities.

**Owner**: Hajime coordinator.

**Partner involvement**: Targeted attorney, accountant, immigration, banking, or other specialist input where planning requires professional judgment.

**Typical duration**: 3-10 business days.

**Deliverables**: Written launch plan, current/next/owner/timeframe summary, open-decision list, and partner-engagement roadmap.

**Dependencies**: Responsive decision-maker, candid business information, and sufficient ownership detail.

**Estimated effort**: Standard; High for complex ownership or regulated activities.

**Automation potential**: Intake normalization, missing-fact detection, route comparison, service recommendation, timeline drafting, and plan-quality checks. Coordinator and professionals approve material conclusions.

**Can it be sold separately?**: Yes. It is the preferred paid diagnostic for clients not ready to purchase a full launch package.

## 2. Business Formation Coordination

**Purpose**: Coordinate the work required to establish the selected Japanese business entity and obtain a complete formation outcome.

**Description**: Hajime manages the project plan, client decisions, professional assignments, evidence requests, address and capital dependencies, document readiness, filing handoff, status tracking, and post-incorporation transition. The filing and legal work is performed by qualified partners.

**Inputs**: Approved launch plan; entity route; founder and stakeholder facts; business activities; capital plan; proposed name; registered-address route; identity evidence; professional engagement.

**Outputs**: Coordinated formation packet readiness; tracked professional filing; verified receipt of incorporation outcome; legal business identifiers recorded; open post-formation actions routed.

**Owner**: Hajime coordinator.

**Partner involvement**: Judicial scrivener, attorney, notary, filing specialist, accountant, translator, and office provider as applicable.

**Typical duration**: 3-10 weeks, with government and partner variance.

**Deliverables**: Formation project plan, consolidated request list, decision log, filing-readiness summary, status updates, incorporation-result handoff, and next-step plan.

**Dependencies**: Final entity facts, usable address, capital evidence, required signatures and certifications, partner acceptance, and client responsiveness.

**Estimated effort**: High.

**Automation potential**: Requirement and task generation, packet readiness, reminders, dependency tracking, timeline updates, and status drafting. Professional review and filing authority remain human-owned.

**Can it be sold separately?**: Yes, but only after readiness planning or equivalent approved scoping.

## 3. Registered Address Coordination

**Purpose**: Help the client secure an address option appropriate for the intended business and formation path.

**Description**: Hajime clarifies operating needs, presents suitable provider options, coordinates availability and terms, routes questions to appropriate professionals, tracks client selection, and confirms required evidence for downstream use. Hajime does not act as a real-estate broker unless separately licensed.

**Inputs**: Target city; entity and activity; staffing and visitor needs; budget range; lease timing; mail and signage needs; registration and banking constraints identified by partners.

**Outputs**: Qualified shortlist; provider comparisons; decision record; provider handoff; address evidence readiness; activation and renewal dates.

**Owner**: Hajime coordinator.

**Partner involvement**: Registered-office provider, serviced-office provider, mail provider, landlord or broker, and attorney or scrivener for suitability questions.

**Typical duration**: 3 business days to 4 weeks.

**Deliverables**: Shortlist, comparison summary, provider introduction, decision checklist, evidence handoff, and renewal owner.

**Dependencies**: Business activity, local-presence needs, provider inventory, professional confirmation where required, and client approval.

**Estimated effort**: Standard.

**Automation potential**: Requirement-based option filtering, availability tracking, comparison preparation, renewal reminders, and downstream evidence routing.

**Can it be sold separately?**: Yes, for qualified business clients. Property search beyond provider coordination requires separate scope and licensed partners.

## 4. Capital Readiness Coordination

**Purpose**: Coordinate the decisions and evidence required for formation capital without providing financial, tax, or legal advice.

**Description**: Hajime translates partner requirements into a client action plan, tracks source and transfer evidence, coordinates timing with formation and banking work, and confirms that the responsible professional has accepted the evidence for the intended use.

**Inputs**: Approved capital amount and currency; contributor identities; source-of-funds context; transfer route; partner evidence requirements; target deposit date.

**Outputs**: Capital action plan; evidence checklist; transfer-timing plan; consolidated questions; requirement-acceptance status; downstream handoff.

**Owner**: Hajime coordinator.

**Partner involvement**: Attorney, scrivener, accountant, tax adviser, bank, or payment provider as appropriate.

**Typical duration**: 1-4 weeks.

**Deliverables**: Capital readiness checklist, evidence index, partner question log, timing updates, and accepted-evidence confirmation.

**Dependencies**: Professional advice on structure where needed, accessible funds, transparent source information, and compliant transfer evidence.

**Estimated effort**: Standard; High for multiple contributors or complex source evidence.

**Automation potential**: Evidence request consolidation, expiry and consistency checks, routing, and dependency impact. No automated financial or legal conclusion.

**Can it be sold separately?**: Normally no. It is an add-on or component of formation coordination.

## 5. Translation and Certification Coordination

**Purpose**: Obtain translations and certifications that meet the requirements of named receiving parties.

**Description**: Hajime identifies source documents, recipient requirements, language pair, certification level, target date, and qualified provider. The coordinator routes versions, tracks review and corrections, and ensures each output is connected to its required use.

**Inputs**: Source documents; target language; receiving partner or agency; required certification, notarization, apostille, or legalization; deadline; formatting expectations.

**Outputs**: Assigned translation or certification work; reviewed deliverables; version manifest; receiving-party acceptance or remediation status.

**Owner**: Hajime coordinator or document specialist.

**Partner involvement**: Translator, certified translator, notary, legalization provider, attorney, embassy, or government office.

**Typical duration**: 2 business days to 4 weeks, depending on volume and certification.

**Deliverables**: Quote and scope comparison, provider assignment, source/output manifest, quality-review checklist, accepted translated or certified document set.

**Dependencies**: Final source version, clear receiving-party standard, provider availability, and physical-original logistics where needed.

**Estimated effort**: Light to High based on volume, languages, and certification path.

**Automation potential**: Language detection, volume estimation, missing-page checks, provider matching, status reminders, and source-output consistency assistance. Certified quality decisions remain qualified work.

**Can it be sold separately?**: Yes, when the receiving purpose and acceptance standard are clear.

## 6. Licensed Professional Introduction and Coordination

**Purpose**: Connect the client with qualified legal, immigration, tax, accounting, or other regulated professionals and coordinate the engagement within the launch plan.

**Description**: Hajime identifies the capability needed, prepares a concise case brief, recommends appropriate vetted partners, coordinates introductions and scheduling, tracks open questions and deliverables, and translates outcomes into project actions. The professional contracts for and owns their advice.

**Inputs**: Project facts; defined professional question or scope; country and region; language; timing; complexity; conflict and budget constraints.

**Outputs**: Partner shortlist or assignment; prepared introduction; scoped question list; meeting preparation; tracked deliverables; coordinated follow-up.

**Owner**: Hajime coordinator; partner manager supports selection.

**Partner involvement**: The selected licensed professional owns professional services and conclusions.

**Typical duration**: Introduction in 2-7 business days; professional work varies by scope.

**Deliverables**: Partner-fit summary, introduction brief, responsibility boundaries, meeting agenda, advice-to-action summary approved for operational use, and escalation route.

**Dependencies**: Partner capacity, conflict clearance, client acceptance, separate engagement terms where required, and adequate project facts.

**Estimated effort**: Light for introduction; Standard or High for ongoing coordination.

**Automation potential**: Capability matching, availability checks, brief preparation, scheduling, and follow-up drafting. Partner selection in material matters remains reviewed.

**Can it be sold separately?**: Introductions may be offered separately only under a clear referral scope. Ongoing coordination is a project or concierge service.

## 7. Government Registration Tracking

**Purpose**: Maintain clear visibility and response readiness while a filing or registration is with a government agency.

**Description**: Hajime records the responsible filer, agency, submission date, expected range, acknowledgments, requests for additional information, and next review point. Hajime coordinates responses but does not influence or guarantee government decisions.

**Inputs**: Filing reference; agency; filing party; submission evidence; published or observed timing; contact and escalation procedure.

**Outputs**: Current status; expected range; next check; delay and risk signal; routed agency correspondence; verified outcome handoff.

**Owner**: Hajime coordinator.

**Partner involvement**: Filing professional or service communicates with the agency and interprets substantive requests.

**Typical duration**: Follows the agency process, commonly several days to several months.

**Deliverables**: Submission acknowledgment record, client-safe updates, request-for-information action plan, escalation log, and result confirmation.

**Dependencies**: Authorized filer, valid submission, reliable acknowledgment route, and partner responsiveness.

**Estimated effort**: Light to Standard; High if remediation is required.

**Automation potential**: Check scheduling, expected-range updates, correspondence classification, reminder suppression, and status drafting. Agency outcomes require authoritative confirmation.

**Can it be sold separately?**: Normally no. It accompanies a coordinated filing or ongoing support engagement.

## 8. Accounting and Tax Setup Coordination

**Purpose**: Establish a clear handoff to qualified accounting and tax providers and coordinate required post-formation registrations and operating decisions.

**Description**: Hajime prepares the business context, sources a suitable accountant or tax partner, schedules the intake, tracks requested evidence, records agreed responsibilities and recurring dates, and confirms that launch-critical setup is complete. Hajime does not provide tax or accounting advice.

**Inputs**: Incorporation evidence; business activities; projected transactions; staffing and payroll plans; fiscal preferences; ownership; existing adviser context; language needs.

**Outputs**: Partner engagement; registration and setup work plan; evidence handoff; recurring-obligation calendar; responsibility map; open-decision list.

**Owner**: Hajime coordinator.

**Partner involvement**: Licensed tax accountant, accountant, bookkeeper, payroll provider, or social-insurance specialist.

**Typical duration**: 1-8 weeks, often parallel with banking.

**Deliverables**: Provider comparison or assignment, prepared intake, registration tracker, accounting-stack handoff, recurring calendar, and accepted operating owner.

**Dependencies**: Business identifiers, operating model, professional availability, client decisions, and formation documents.

**Estimated effort**: Standard.

**Automation potential**: Intake preparation, obligation checklist drafting, document reuse suggestions, deadline reminders, and handoff completeness. Tax elections and advice remain professional decisions.

**Can it be sold separately?**: Yes, after incorporation or as a launch add-on.

## 9. Bank Introduction and Readiness Coordination

**Purpose**: Prepare the client for a suitable business banking path and coordinate introductions, evidence, interviews, and follow-up.

**Description**: Hajime compares candidate banking paths based on known eligibility, prepares the business narrative and evidence index, coordinates introductions or applications where available, prepares the client for meetings, and routes additional requests. Hajime does not guarantee approval or act as a bank.

**Inputs**: Incorporation and tax records; ownership; identity; address; business plan; capital source; local activity; expected transactions; desired services; language and interview availability.

**Outputs**: Banking-route recommendation for client decision; readiness gap list; prepared introduction; evidence packet; interview preparation; tracked requests and outcome.

**Owner**: Hajime coordinator or banking-readiness specialist.

**Partner involvement**: Bank, banking introduction partner, accountant, attorney, or translator.

**Typical duration**: 2-12 weeks or longer at bank discretion.

**Deliverables**: Bank-path comparison, readiness report, evidence index, introduction brief, interview agenda, follow-up tracker, and alternate-path recommendation if needed.

**Dependencies**: Formed business or bank-eligible stage, transparent ownership and funds, credible business activity, required local presence, and bank willingness to consider the client.

**Estimated effort**: Standard to High.

**Automation potential**: Readiness checks, evidence matching, interview preparation, request routing, and timeline updates. No automated approval prediction presented as certainty.

**Can it be sold separately?**: Yes for qualified formed businesses, with a clear no-guarantee acknowledgment.

## 10. Insurance Setup Coordination

**Purpose**: Connect the business with appropriate insurance professionals and coordinate the information and timing needed for coverage decisions.

**Description**: Hajime identifies operational insurance needs at a category level, prepares business facts, introduces qualified brokers or insurers, tracks quotes and questions, and confirms selected coverage activation. Advice and suitability remain with licensed providers and the client.

**Inputs**: Business activities; employees; locations; contracts; assets; revenue range; customer requirements; launch date; prior coverage.

**Outputs**: Provider introduction; quote process; comparison framework; tracked decision; evidence of activation; renewal owner.

**Owner**: Hajime coordinator.

**Partner involvement**: Licensed insurance broker, agent, or insurer.

**Typical duration**: 1-4 weeks.

**Deliverables**: Prepared intake, provider introduction, quote tracker, decision checklist, activation confirmation, and renewal date.

**Dependencies**: Sufficient business facts, qualified provider availability, underwriting, and client selection.

**Estimated effort**: Light to Standard.

**Automation potential**: Intake preparation, missing-information detection, quote-status tracking, activation and renewal reminders. Coverage advice remains licensed work.

**Can it be sold separately?**: Yes for existing business clients, subject to partner coverage.

## 11. Operational Launch Coordination

**Purpose**: Move the newly formed business from legal existence to launch-ready operations.

**Description**: Hajime coordinates the remaining launch-critical handoffs across address, mail, banking, accounting, payroll, insurance, records, credentials, and recurring obligations. The service ends with a clear operating owner for every continuing responsibility.

**Inputs**: Formation outcome; package scope; operating plan; staffing; provider assignments; open registrations; launch date; client operating team.

**Outputs**: Operational-readiness assessment; completed provider handoffs; credentials and records inventory; unresolved-risk plan; recurring-responsibility calendar.

**Owner**: Hajime coordinator.

**Partner involvement**: Accountant, bank, insurer, office and mail provider, payroll provider, attorney, and other operating specialists.

**Typical duration**: 2-8 weeks, overlapping formation and registration where possible.

**Deliverables**: Readiness checklist, provider directory, responsibility map, operating packet, launch review, and accepted handoff.

**Dependencies**: Incorporation outcome, provider availability, client operating decisions, and access to required records.

**Estimated effort**: High.

**Automation potential**: Readiness evaluation, handoff tracking, packet assembly, recurring task generation, and gap detection.

**Can it be sold separately?**: Yes for recently formed businesses after a paid readiness review.

## 12. Post-Launch Concierge

**Purpose**: Give the client one coordinating contact for operational changes, provider handoffs, and emerging administrative needs after launch.

**Description**: Hajime triages requests, determines the responsible provider, prepares context, coordinates the work, tracks outcomes, and maintains the operating responsibility map. Requests requiring new material scope are quoted separately.

**Inputs**: Active business profile; provider network; support request; current obligations; authorized client contacts; service limits.

**Outputs**: Triage decision; assigned owner; coordinated resolution; updated records and upcoming obligations; escalation when professional advice is required.

**Owner**: Ongoing-service coordinator.

**Partner involvement**: Any qualified operating partner needed for the request.

**Typical duration**: Recurring monthly or annual engagement; individual requests vary.

**Deliverables**: Named coordinator, support-response standard, monthly status or digest, request tracker, provider handoffs, and quarterly operating review where included.

**Dependencies**: Active retainer, defined response scope, current business information, and partner availability.

**Estimated effort**: Variable; controlled through retainer tier and request limits.

**Automation potential**: Request classification, routing, recurring reminders, provider brief drafting, and digest generation. Material advice and scope changes remain reviewed.

**Can it be sold separately?**: Yes after a paid onboarding and records review.

## 13. Annual Compliance Coordination

**Purpose**: Coordinate recurring corporate, tax, address, document, insurance, and provider obligations so they have clear owners and deadlines.

**Description**: Hajime maintains a validated annual calendar, confirms responsible partners, requests evidence and decisions in advance, tracks completion, and escalates missing work. Hajime does not make legal or tax determinations; qualified partners validate applicable obligations.

**Inputs**: Business and ownership profile; fiscal calendar; provider engagements; prior filings; address and officer records; licenses; insurance; country and activity requirements validated by professionals.

**Outputs**: Annual obligation calendar; assigned tasks; partner handoffs; completion evidence; overdue and risk escalation; year-end review.

**Owner**: Compliance coordinator.

**Partner involvement**: Accountant, tax professional, attorney, scrivener, insurer, office provider, payroll provider, and government filing specialists.

**Typical duration**: Annual recurring engagement with work throughout the year.

**Deliverables**: Validated calendar, responsibility matrix, reminders, completion tracker, evidence index, quarterly or annual review, and next-year rollover.

**Dependencies**: Current business facts, professional validation, active provider relationships, and timely client responses.

**Estimated effort**: Standard recurring; High for multi-entity or regulated clients.

**Automation potential**: Recurring task generation, deadline reminders, document-expiry monitoring, completion tracking, and escalation. Applicability and filings remain professionally owned.

**Can it be sold separately?**: Yes after an initial compliance and records review.

## 14. Enterprise Launch Program

**Purpose**: Coordinate multiple launches, entities, or countries under one governance, reporting, and service framework.

**Description**: Hajime designs a program-level plan, standard intake, country workstreams, partner coverage, central reporting, escalation governance, procurement support, and executive reviews. Each local workstream still uses local partners and boundaries.

**Inputs**: Expansion portfolio; target countries; entities and activities; governance; procurement requirements; timelines; central and local stakeholders; reporting needs.

**Outputs**: Program roadmap; country workstreams; common controls; partner matrix; executive reporting; consolidated risk and dependency view; operational handoffs.

**Owner**: Senior program coordinator or account lead.

**Partner involvement**: Country-specific professional and operating partners, plus enterprise procurement and legal stakeholders.

**Typical duration**: Custom, commonly several months to a multi-year program.

**Deliverables**: Program charter, launch portfolio, governance cadence, country plans, consolidated reporting, escalation framework, and handoff standards.

**Dependencies**: Executive sponsor, country readiness, procurement, partner coverage, standardized decision authority, and realistic sequencing.

**Estimated effort**: Custom.

**Automation potential**: Portfolio reporting, template reuse, cross-country document reuse suggestions, capacity planning, and risk aggregation. Country-specific professional decisions remain local.

**Can it be sold separately?**: Yes, as a custom enterprise engagement.

## Catalog governance

Each service has an internal owner responsible for scope clarity, delivery standard, partner coverage, effort assumptions, quality measures, and boundary language. New services begin as limited pilots.

A service may enter general sale only when Hajime has:

- A clear client outcome and boundary.
- Repeatable inputs, outputs, and acceptance criteria.
- At least one primary and one contingency delivery path for critical partner capabilities.
- Tested duration and coordinator-effort ranges.
- Approved client wording and escalation rules.
- Commercial terms that cover coordination and exception cost.

Scope changes are documented rather than absorbed invisibly. Repeated custom requests should become either a defined add-on, a revised service, or an explicit non-service.
