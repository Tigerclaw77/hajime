# Hajime Japan Service Packages

## Package philosophy

Hajime should sell four understandable levels of support. Customers should not need to compare dozens of line items before they understand the difference.

The distinction is the degree of coordination Hajime owns:

- **DIY Blueprint**: Hajime creates the plan; the client runs it.
- **Guided Launch**: Hajime guides the client and coordinates key handoffs.
- **Concierge**: Hajime actively manages the launch across providers.
- **Enterprise**: Hajime governs a customized portfolio or complex expansion.

Professional fees, government fees, banking costs, rent, insurance premiums, translation, notarization, and other external costs are listed separately unless a written proposal expressly includes them.

Package names describe service intensity, not likelihood of government, bank, visa, or professional approval.

## DIY Blueprint

### Who it is for

Prepared founders with a straightforward business, ample time, comfort managing local providers, and a desire for a validated plan before proceeding independently.

### Included services

- Launch Strategy and Readiness Planning.
- Structured business and founder intake.
- Responsibility and dependency map.
- Preliminary timeline range.
- Provider-capability recommendations.
- Consolidated launch checklist.
- One plan-review session and a limited clarification window.

### Not included

- Ongoing project management.
- Partner sourcing, introduction, or assignment management beyond defined recommendations.
- Document review or routing.
- Government registration tracking.
- Bank, accounting, office, translation, insurance, or operational coordination.
- Professional advice or third-party fees.
- Replanning after material changes unless purchased separately.

### Typical timeline

The blueprint is normally delivered within 5-10 business days after complete intake. The client's total launch timeline depends on their execution and providers.

### Ideal client

A founder who can confidently manage email, follow-up, scheduling, evidence, and provider selection and primarily needs a reliable route map.

### Upgrade path

Upgrade to Guided Launch when the client wants active checkpoints and introductions. Upgrade to Concierge when the client wants Hajime to own day-to-day coordination. A Blueprint fee may be partially credited when the upgrade occurs promptly and the underlying assumptions remain valid.

## Guided Launch

### Who it is for

Hands-on founders who want a trusted guide, qualified introductions, and structured review while retaining responsibility for most provider communication and action follow-up.

### Included services

- Everything in DIY Blueprint.
- Named Hajime coordinator.
- Business Formation Coordination with defined limits.
- Licensed Professional Introduction and Coordination for core formation roles.
- Registered Address Coordination shortlist and handoff.
- Consolidated document and decision requests.
- Scheduled progress reviews.
- Timeline and government-registration tracking.
- Accounting and Tax Setup Coordination handoff.
- Bank readiness orientation and one banking path, subject to eligibility.
- Launch handoff summary.

### Not included

- Unlimited coordinator communication or ad hoc operational requests.
- Hajime managing every partner conversation.
- Complex immigration, licensing, regulated activity, multi-founder remediation, or ownership restructuring.
- Multiple simultaneous banking applications.
- Extensive translation or certification work.
- Post-launch concierge or annual compliance unless added.
- Professional advice and third-party fees unless expressly included.

### Typical timeline

Commonly 6-14 weeks for a straightforward formation and launch handoff. Government, bank, professional, and client waiting can extend the range.

### Ideal client

A responsive founder who wants reliable structure and periodic intervention but is comfortable completing actions and communicating directly with assigned partners.

### Upgrade path

Upgrade to Concierge when the founder lacks time, the project gains complexity, several partners must move in parallel, or proactive daily coordination becomes necessary. Add Post-Launch Concierge or Annual Compliance Coordination after launch.

## Concierge

### Who it is for

Founders and expansion teams who value time, discretion, proactive management, and one accountable relationship across the entire launch.

### Included services

- Everything in Guided Launch.
- Active end-to-end project management.
- Priority coordinator response standard.
- Partner sourcing, assignment, brief preparation, and handoff management.
- Registered Address Coordination.
- Capital Readiness Coordination.
- Standard Translation and Certification Coordination management, with external fees identified separately.
- Government Registration Tracking and response coordination.
- Accounting and Tax Setup Coordination.
- Bank Introduction and Readiness Coordination for the agreed path.
- Insurance Setup Coordination when appropriate.
- Operational Launch Coordination.
- Prepared meetings, consolidated action requests, proactive escalation, and client-safe weekly updates.
- Complete operating handoff packet.
- Limited post-launch stabilization period.

### Not included

- Guaranteed formation, banking, immigration, tax, licensing, or government outcomes.
- Professional services delivered by Hajime.
- Material business restructuring, disputed matters, investigations, litigation, or regulated-industry licensing unless separately scoped.
- Unlimited translations, applications, bank paths, founders, entities, or geographic locations.
- Ongoing annual compliance after the stabilization period.
- Third-party fees unless expressly included.

### Typical timeline

Commonly 8-18 weeks for a standard launch, with a tailored range after planning. Work runs in parallel where responsible and possible.

### Ideal client

A busy international founder, executive, or investor who wants Hajime to carry the coordination burden and intervene early when work stalls.

### Upgrade path

Add ongoing Post-Launch Concierge and Annual Compliance Coordination. Move to Enterprise when the engagement includes multiple entities, countries, procurement, centralized reporting, regulated complexity, or repeated launches.

## Enterprise

### Who it is for

Companies, funds, accelerators, family offices, and expansion teams managing complex, repeated, or multi-market launches with governance and reporting requirements.

### Included services

Enterprise is custom-scoped from the service catalog and commonly includes:

- Senior account or program lead.
- Enterprise Launch Program.
- Multiple project and country workstreams.
- Central governance and executive reporting.
- Procurement and contracting coordination.
- Partner-capacity planning and approved provider panels.
- Custom service levels and escalation paths.
- Standardized intake, deliverables, and operating handoffs.
- Portfolio risk, dependency, and launch-date reporting.
- Ongoing concierge or compliance coordination.

### Not included

- Any unlicensed professional service by Hajime.
- Guaranteed external outcomes.
- Unsupported countries, activities, or provider categories.
- Unbounded scope, unlimited entities, or unlimited stakeholder support.
- Professional and external fees not expressly included in the commercial schedule.

### Typical timeline

Defined through a program roadmap. Initial design and country readiness normally require several weeks before execution. Programs may run for months or years.

### Ideal client

An organization with an executive sponsor, repeatable expansion needs, several stakeholders, defined governance, and sufficient volume to benefit from a centralized coordination layer.

### Upgrade path

Enterprise expands through additional countries, entities, ongoing operations, compliance coordination, reporting, and dedicated capacity. New scope is added through an agreed program change, not informal package drift.

## Optional add-ons

Add-ons should remain few and outcome-based:

- Additional founder, shareholder, or beneficial-owner coordination.
- Additional banking path.
- Expanded translation and certification coordination.
- Complex office or location search coordination.
- Immigration professional coordination.
- Regulated-activity or licensing coordination.
- Accelerated recovery or remediation after rejection.
- Post-Launch Concierge.
- Annual Compliance Coordination.
- Additional entity or project.

An add-on covers Hajime coordination. Professional work and external charges remain separately identified.

## Package-change rules

1. Upgrade when complexity changes the operating model, not simply because a task is inconvenient.
2. Present the scope difference and fee before starting added work whenever timing permits.
3. Do not downgrade after substantial included work has been performed; instead remove clearly future scope through a written change.
4. Do not hide professional or third-party costs inside vague “all-inclusive” language.
5. A coordinator cannot promise work outside package boundaries to resolve client dissatisfaction. Escalate and amend scope.
6. Package exceptions become explicit proposal terms and do not silently redefine the standard package.
