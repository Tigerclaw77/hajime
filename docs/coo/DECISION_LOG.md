# Hajime COO Decision Log

**Owner:** COO  
**Last updated:** 2026-07-12

This log records major operating recommendations. Decisions remain active until their review trigger is reached or a later entry supersedes them.

## Active Decisions

### D-001: Make customer acquisition the only P0

- **Decision:** Protect founder time for warm introductions, qualified discovery, paid offers, and partner conversations. Pause nonessential engineering and strategy work.
- **Reasoning:** Hajime has extensive planning and no recorded paid-customer baseline. Payment evidence is now the highest-value missing information.
- **Expected outcome:** First paying customer within two weeks and five paying customers during Q3 2026.
- **Date:** 2026-07-12.
- **Success criteria:** One signed, paid, scheduled customer; then five paying customers with positive contribution margin.
- **Future review date:** 2026-07-31 or after six qualified offers, whichever comes first.
- **Disposition:** **Manual First**.

### D-002: Lead with paid Japan Launch Readiness

- **Decision:** Use Japan Launch Readiness as the smallest paid entry offer, initially testing USD 1,500 under a fixed written scope.
- **Reasoning:** It tests willingness to pay, qualifies larger work, and can be delivered manually without software or an oversized partner network.
- **Expected outcome:** Faster commitment, clearer qualification, and evidence for package pricing.
- **Date:** 2026-07-12.
- **Success criteria:** At least 30% acceptance among qualified comparable offers, median customer value rating >=8/10, and positive contribution margin.
- **Future review date:** After five comparable offers or Customer 3, whichever comes first.
- **Disposition:** **Manual First**.

### D-003: Prioritize professional referrals over paid acquisition

- **Decision:** Pilot non-exclusive referral relationships with an accountant, judicial scrivener, administrative scrivener/immigration professional, and international business attorney before buying traffic.
- **Reasoning:** These partners encounter triggered customers, confer trust, and can reduce acquisition cost while improving delivery preparation.
- **Expected outcome:** Two qualified referrals and one paying customer from four 60-day pilots.
- **Date:** 2026-07-12.
- **Success criteria:** At least two qualified referrals, one paid conversion, and one partner making a second referral.
- **Future review date:** 2026-09-11 or after the second partner referral.
- **Disposition:** **Partner**.

### D-004: Keep referral commissions out of initial validation

- **Decision:** Use no paid referral commissions for the first ten customers.
- **Reasoning:** Customer-paid coordination cleanly tests Hajime's value and avoids trust, professional-rule, and recommendation-bias concerns.
- **Expected outcome:** Unambiguous revenue validation and neutral partner selection.
- **Date:** 2026-07-12.
- **Success criteria:** Ten customers acquired without commission-dependent ranking; no material conflict complaint.
- **Future review date:** After Customer 10 and Japanese counsel review.
- **Disposition:** **Ignore** commission opportunities until the review gate.

### D-005: Require manual evidence before software investment

- **Decision:** Do not build product features until at least five customers have paid and a repeated acquisition or delivery bottleneck has a measured founder-hour cost.
- **Reasoning:** Engineering time is scarce, and manual delivery is sufficient to expose the actual constraint. Building earlier risks automating assumptions.
- **Expected outcome:** Lower wasted development, clearer ROI, and automation directed at proven work.
- **Date:** 2026-07-12.
- **Success criteria:** Every proposed feature has an Investment Memo, measured baseline, manual workaround, ROI hypothesis, and kill criteria.
- **Future review date:** After Customer 5.
- **Disposition:** **Manual First**.

### D-006: Sequence market expansion behind operating gates

- **Decision:** Focus on triggered founder-led technology, professional-services, venture-backed, and profitable SMB customers before Wave 2 segments or another country.
- **Reasoning:** These customers share the core workflow, have reachable decision-makers, and create the fastest path to premium revenue without new vertical operating systems.
- **Expected outcome:** Repeatable Wave 1 delivery and a defensible partner network before complexity increases.
- **Date:** 2026-07-12.
- **Success criteria:** Ten paid customers, positive margin, repeat channel, repeat partner referral, and median value rating >=8/10 before Wave 2.
- **Future review date:** After Customer 10.
- **Disposition:** **Manual First** for Wave 1; **Ignore** expansion work this quarter.

### D-007: Establish a manual COO operating cadence

- **Decision:** Maintain `EXECUTIVE_DASHBOARD.md` weekly and this decision log for major recommendations. Do not build dashboard software.
- **Reasoning:** A single operating view reduces founder attention loss and makes priorities, risks, metrics, and stop/go gates explicit at minimal cost.
- **Expected outcome:** Weekly commercial accountability and fewer low-leverage initiatives.
- **Date:** 2026-07-12.
- **Success criteria:** Dashboard updated weekly in <=30 minutes; no more than three active weekly priorities; major recommendations logged before execution.
- **Future review date:** 2026-08-09.
- **Disposition:** **Manual First**; later **Delegate** maintenance if the cadence proves useful.

### D-008: Delegate Japanese legal and boundary review

- **Decision:** Use qualified Japanese counsel to review public terms, boundary language, comparative claims, and any future referral economics.
- **Reasoning:** This work reduces material customer-trust and regulatory risk and should not be improvised by the founder or software team.
- **Expected outcome:** Clear commercial boundaries and lower risk of misleading or unauthorized claims.
- **Date:** 2026-07-12.
- **Success criteria:** Written counsel review completed before public customer terms or referral compensation launches.
- **Future review date:** 2026-08-31 or before the first public commercial launch, whichever comes first.
- **Disposition:** **Delegate**.
