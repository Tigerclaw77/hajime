# Hajime Assumption Register

## Evidence standard

The register treats research, competitor activity, interviews, and stated interest as weak evidence. Cleared payment, observed customer behavior, measured delivery economics, repeat purchases, and repeat referrals are strong evidence.

Importance:

- **Critical:** Failure invalidates or materially changes the business.
- **High:** Failure damages economics, distribution, or scalability.
- **Medium:** Failure removes an expansion path but not the core business.

Current confidence is deliberately conservative because no reliable paid-customer baseline is recorded.

## Assumption priority

Validate in this order:

1. **Payment:** A01, A02, A03, A05.
2. **Reach and acquisition:** A04, A07, A08.
3. **Delivery and economics:** A10, A11, A12, A13, A14, A17, A18, A20.
4. **Expansion economics:** A06, A09, A15, A16, A19.

Do not spend time validating lower-order assumptions while payment remains unknown.

## Business-level failure gate

Recommend no further investment when any remains true after the 60-90 day program:

- Fewer than 5 of 30 qualified prospects pay.
- Fewer than 3 customers will pay at least $1,500.
- No source produces a second customer.
- Contribution margin remains non-positive after one controlled correction.
- Manual delivery cannot remain within professional boundaries.
- Customer value rating remains below 6/10 after one delivery correction.
