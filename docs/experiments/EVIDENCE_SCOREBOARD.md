# Hajime Evidence Scoreboard

## Purpose

Replace narrative confidence with evidence. An assumption moves only when a named experiment produces the required result. Time spent, documents created, meetings held, and features completed do not improve an evidence grade by themselves.

## Evidence grades

| Grade | Meaning | Permitted evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Unknown** | No Hajime-specific behavioral evidence | Research and opinions may exist, but no relevant observed test |
| **Weak Evidence** | Indirect or small-sample signal | Competitor activity, interviews, stated intent, one behavior without payment |
| **Moderate Evidence** | Repeated relevant behavior | One or two payments, several qualified behaviors, or one delivered case |
| **Strong Evidence** | Repeated paid and operational evidence | Multiple payments, repeat channel behavior, measured delivery outcomes |
| **Validated** | Precommitted pass threshold reached | Assumption's pass criteria met at required sample/window |
| **Invalidated** | Precommitted fail threshold reached | Assumption's fail criteria met; investment decision changes |

Evidence does not automatically progress in order. A clean fail can move an assumption directly from Unknown to Invalidated.

## Evidence promotion rules

### Payment assumptions

- One payment: Moderate Evidence.
- Three unrelated payments at the tested price: Strong Evidence.
- Pass sample and margin threshold reached: Validated.
- Fail sample reached with zero payment: Invalidated.

### Channel assumptions

- Positive replies or meetings: Weak Evidence.
- One qualified referral or paid customer: Moderate Evidence.
- Repeat paid customers from the same source: Strong Evidence.
- Precommitted repeatability threshold reached: Validated.

### Delivery assumptions

- Completed free or internal simulation: Weak Evidence.
- One paid delivered case: Moderate Evidence.
- Three or more paid cases with consistent value/margin: Strong Evidence.
- Required sample and target reached: Validated.

### Trust and boundary assumptions

- Written policy: Weak Evidence.
- Customer/partner comprehension in a live case: Moderate Evidence.
- Repeated clean cases plus professional review: Strong Evidence.
- Counsel-approved model and pass sample without material incident: Validated.

## Portfolio-level evidence gates

### Gate 1: Continue first-customer validation

- At least one payment from six qualified offers.
- Customer accepts the coordination boundary.

Otherwise invalidate the current offer or segment; do not add marketing volume.

### Gate 2: Continue toward a repeatable business

- Five paying customers.
- At least three at $1,500+ or larger paid deposits.
- Two customers from one source.
- Positive contribution margin.
- Median value rating >=8/10.

Otherwise pivot one variable or stop.

### Gate 3: Permit selective reinvestment

- Ten paying customers.
- Proposal acceptance >=35%.
- Median close <=45 days.
- One partner makes a second qualified referral.
- A non-founder can follow the manual workflow or the founder labor bottleneck is precisely measured.
- No material boundary incident.

Only this gate permits consideration of meaningful engineering or channel spend. It does not automatically approve either.

## Scoreboard discipline

- Update after each experiment, not after every conversation.
- Link raw records rather than summarizing from memory.
- Keep failed and disconfirming evidence visible.
- Do not average incompatible segments or offers.
- Mark insufficient sample as such; do not call it a trend.
- Never upgrade evidence because more planning was completed.
