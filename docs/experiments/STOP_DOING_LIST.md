# Hajime Stop Doing List

## Rule

Avoid any work that consumes founder attention faster than it reduces a material business uncertainty. "Useful someday" is not a reason to act.

## Deliberate non-priorities

| Item | Why it is tempting | Why it should wait | Evidence required before revisiting |

## Stop behaviors

- Counting interviews, signups, proposals, partner meetings, or verbal interest as customers.
- Changing segment, message, scope, and price simultaneously.
- Extending a failed test without changing an explicit variable.
- Adding experiment metrics after results are known.
- Accepting unqualified customers to improve conversion numbers.
- Omitting founder labor from margin.
- Treating waiting on a bank, government, or partner as free operational work.
- Recommending providers based on compensation.
- Creating urgency from fear or implying guaranteed outcomes.
- Using "premium" to justify price without measurable burden reduction.

## Revisit protocol

Before restarting any paused item, document:

1. The evidence gate that has been met.
2. The business decision the work will change.
3. The smallest testable version.
4. Expected founder hours, cash cost, and payback.
5. What will be stopped to create capacity.
6. Kill criteria and review date.

If no current experiment or paid customer evidence requires the work, keep it stopped.
