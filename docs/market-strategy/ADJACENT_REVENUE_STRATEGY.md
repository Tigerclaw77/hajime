# Hajime Japan Adjacent Revenue Strategy

## Strategic rule

Adjacent revenue may deepen the launch relationship, but must never turn Hajime into an advertising platform. Customer-paid coordination remains the primary revenue. A partner opportunity is acceptable only when it solves a repeated customer problem, preserves choice, discloses economics, and stays inside Hajime's professional boundaries.

Priority meanings:

- **Core:** Directly strengthens formation coordination or a complete operating handoff.
- **Secondary:** Valuable after the first launch workflows and governance are proven.
- **Future:** Potentially useful, but operationally distracting or remote from the initial promise.
- **Reject:** More likely to dilute trust or positioning than create durable value.

Revenue potential and implementation difficulty ratings are 1 (low) to 5 (high). Conflict risk is 1 (low) to 5 (high).

## Core opportunities

### Paid launch readiness

- **Revenue model:** Fixed customer fee, potentially partially credited to a larger engagement under pre-defined terms.
- **Why core:** It qualifies serious customers and is useful even when the answer is to stop or use a specialist directly.
- **Guardrail:** Professional conclusions remain with licensed advisers.

### Post-launch coordination

- **Revenue model:** Fixed stabilization project or monthly/quarterly retainer with defined request limits.
- **Why core:** It extends "what happens next?" into annual obligations, provider changes, and operational handoffs.
- **Guardrail:** No anxiety-selling or artificial tasks; professional delivery remains separate.

### Office and address coordination

- **Revenue model:** Customer-paid coordination. A disclosed referral arrangement may be tested later if lawful and not ranking-dependent.
- **Why core:** Address decisions can affect formation, bank readiness, immigration, operations, and credibility.
- **Guardrail:** Show reasonable alternatives and route suitability questions to qualified parties.

### Translation and certification coordination

- **Revenue model:** Fixed coordination fee plus transparent external costs, or direct customer/provider contract.
- **Why core:** Version control, context, and evidence routing are genuine coordination work.
- **Guardrail:** Do not hide markups or imply Hajime certifies documents.

## Secondary opportunities

### Employment, payroll, and benefits

These strengthen the employer-entity entry path and create recurring provider relationships. Revenue should initially come from the customer's Hajime coordination scope, not payroll commissions. Add economics only after legal/conflict review and proof that recommendations remain neutral.

### Accounting and software

Accounting handoff is essential; accounting software affiliate income is not. Recommend the stack selected by customer needs and the responsible accountant. A customer-paid onboarding coordination fee is cleaner than provider-funded placement.

### Banking readiness

Hajime may coordinate preparation, introductions, status, and alternative paths. It must not recommend a bank because of payment, share data without consent, or tie compensation to account approval.

### Insurance

Introduce a qualified broker at the correct milestone. Hajime does not determine coverage or act as broker. All compensation must be reviewed and disclosed.

### Coworking, mail, customs, shipping, and fulfillment

These become strategically meaningful when tied to a real entry path. Commerce providers should be activated by product and operating fit, not affiliate payout.

## Future opportunities

- **Telecommunications:** Useful for physical operations, but too small to lead with.
- **Housing/relocation:** High customer value for Concierge customers, but requires separate privacy, real-estate, and personal-service boundaries.
- **Recruiting:** Strong potential LTV, but large referral fees can bias recommendations and recruiting is a different service category.
- **Hotels:** Offer only when a relocating customer requests help. Present a few fit-based options, not a booking catalog.

## Rejected opportunities

- Airline and generic travel affiliate pages.
- Sponsored provider rankings.
- Lead resale or undisclosed data sharing.
- Approval-linked banking, visa, license, or government commissions.
- White-labeled legal, tax, accounting, insurance, financial, or real-estate advice.
- "Member benefits" catalogs unrelated to a current launch problem.

These create revenue without strengthening the formation-coordination capability and therefore dilute the brand.

## Revenue activation sequence

### First 10 customers

- Customer-paid readiness, Guided Launch, Concierge, and explicitly scoped coordination only.
- No paid referral program.
- Track every adjacent request and customer value.

### Customers 11-25

- Add post-launch coordination.
- Test office/address and translation coordination as clear customer-paid add-ons.
- Add commerce and employment provider routes only for proven entry paths.

### Customers 26-50

- Consider no more than two disclosed referral arrangements where customers receive a concrete benefit.
- Measure opt-out, alternatives shown, complaints, attachment, margin, and provider performance.
- Keep adjacent revenue reported separately.

### After 50 customers

- Expand recurring post-launch coordination.
- Add selected country/vertical provider ecosystems.
- Continue rejecting ad inventory even if traffic grows.

## Approval gate

An adjacent revenue opportunity is approved only when:

1. At least three customers independently report the underlying problem.
2. It saves measurable time, cost, or risk.
3. Customer choice is preserved.
4. Compensation can be disclosed in one plain sentence.
5. The responsible professional boundary is clear.
6. It has a positive expected contribution after support and governance cost.
7. It does not displace capacity needed for core launches.
8. Japanese counsel and relevant professional-rule owners approve the arrangement where required.

The first question is always "Does this solve the customer's launch problem?" Revenue is considered only after the answer is yes.
