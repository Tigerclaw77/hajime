### Disqualifiers

- [ ] Cheapest filing is the sole criterion.
- [ ] Prospect requires a guaranteed bank, visa, registration, tax, license, or government outcome.
- [ ] Ownership, funds, activity, or identity is concealed.
- [ ] Required deadline is impossible.
- [ ] Primary need is regulated advice or representation.
- [ ] Need is unsupported or outside Hajime's current boundaries.

### Outcome

Choose one:

- [ ] **Strong Fit:** Triggered, multi-workstream, decision-maker engaged, budget evidence present, and no disqualifier.
- [ ] **Nurture:** Credible prospect but no active date or budget; follow up only on a named trigger.
- [ ] **Refer:** A single professional need is clear and coordination adds little value.
- [ ] **Decline:** A disqualifier applies or Hajime cannot responsibly serve the need.
