# Hajime Japan Competitor Database

## Scope and interpretation

Reviewed 2026-07-12. Public offers change; confirm scope and pricing directly before using this database in sales material. "Weakness" means a gap relative to Hajime's proposed coordination job, not a claim about service quality.

The market has five competitive layers:

1. Free public support and self-service tools.
2. Licensed local specialists selling formation or immigration work.
3. English-first bundled Japan setup services.
4. Market-entry and accounting firms.
5. Global corporate-service platforms.

Hajime should not attempt to out-license specialists, underprice software, or out-scale global providers. Its position is **independent, founder-level launch orchestration across the layers**.

## Competitive strengths Hajime must respect

- **Government programs reduce information scarcity.** General checklists and procedural explanations cannot be the premium product.
- **Licensed specialists own regulated credibility.** Hajime should make their role more effective, never obscure it.
- **Bundled providers reduce vendor count.** Hajime must prove that independent coordination offers better clarity, fit, and customer advocacy than one provider's bundle.
- **Global firms offer continuity after launch.** Hajime needs a clean handoff and optional post-launch coordination, even if it does not deliver every recurring service.
- **Transparent startups are converging on the same message.** Interr already combines company, visa, housing, employees, and tax with a package builder. "One-stop" alone is not differentiation.

## Defensible gap

Hajime's strongest gap is a deliberately narrow promise:

> One accountable launch coordinator for commercially committed founders, working with the customer's chosen licensed professionals and making every owner, dependency, request, and next action clear.

This is defensible only if delivery proves:

- Faster readiness and fewer repeated requests.
- Better-prepared professional meetings.
- One consolidated source of truth.
- Proactive delay escalation.
- Transparent provider fees and disclosed commercial relationships.
- A premium human experience without pretending Hajime provides licensed advice.
