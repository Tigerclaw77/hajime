# Hajime Japan Customer Acquisition Experiments

## Objective

Acquire evidence of paid demand before significant additional engineering. The primary metric is not traffic, leads, or interview compliments. It is **qualified prospects who pay for launch readiness or place a deposit on Guided Launch or Concierge**.

Run experiments manually with the existing materials. Do not build acquisition software.

## Qualification standard

A qualified lead must have:

- A company or credible funded venture.
- A concrete Japan trigger within 12 months.
- At least three interdependent launch needs.
- A reachable decision-maker.
- Budget for Hajime plus professional and government costs.
- A need for coordination rather than regulated advice alone.

## Experiment protocols

### 1. Thirty conversations, with a paid ask

Recruit 20 prospective customers and 10 potential referral partners. Use warm introductions first, then targeted outreach. Do not pitch during the first half of a customer interview. At the end, describe a paid launch-readiness deliverable and ask whether they would buy it for a concrete price.

Test three prices with otherwise similar prospects: USD 750, USD 1,500, and USD 2,500. The deliverable should include intake, readiness assessment, dependency map, preliminary cost/timeline ranges, responsibility map, and one review session. Do not discount in exchange for praise. A deposit is evidence; "keep me posted" is not.

**Advance when:** at least 3 of 20 qualified prospects pay or commit to a dated paid engagement.

**Stop or revise when:** prospects value advice from one specialist but do not value independent coordination.

### 2. Professional referral pilot

Target bilingual accountants, judicial scriveners, administrative scriveners, attorneys, and international tax advisers who serve foreign founders but do not want to manage office, bank-readiness, housing, translation, and cross-provider follow-up.

Offer a 60-day no-fee pilot:

- Hajime sends well-qualified, well-prepared clients.
- The professional retains scope control and contracts directly for regulated work.
- Hajime consolidates non-professional requests and protects the specialist's time.
- Both parties use a written handoff and escalation protocol.
- No referral payment is required for validation.

**Advance when:** three professionals agree, two make qualified referrals, and both client and professional report less coordination burden.

### 3. Trigger-based LinkedIn outreach

Build a list manually from public company announcements and profiles. Valid signals include "launching in Japan," Japanese customer or distributor announcements, Tokyo hiring, accelerator acceptance, or founder relocation. Send a short note tied to the signal.

Example:

> Congratulations on the Japan partnership. I coordinate the operational launch for founder-led companies that need entity, office, tax, banking, and specialist work to move in the right order. I am interviewing companies at this exact stage. Would a 20-minute comparison of your current plan be useful?

Do not automate or send generic volume. Log segment, trigger, reply, discovery, proposal, outcome, and objection in a simple sheet.

### 4. Single-offer landing page

Use one page and one conversion path: **Book a Japan Launch Readiness Session**. The page should state the target customer, concrete deliverable, price, boundaries, and what happens after purchase. Avoid a general "we help anyone start in Japan" message.

Traffic should come only from direct outreach, partners, communities, and later high-intent search. Measure qualified conversion separately from all visitors.

### 5. Accelerator and founder office hours

Offer private "Japan launch readiness office hours" to accelerators, venture studios, and founder communities. The host should nominate companies with actual Japan activity. Avoid a broad incorporation seminar; free public information already exists through JETRO and Tokyo programs ([JETRO Invest Japan](https://www.jetro.go.jp/en/invest/support_programs/incentive/offices/), [Invest Tokyo](https://www.startup-support.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/en/)).

Start with B2B programs and networks. Alchemist Japan's enterprise-technology focus is an example of a concentrated audience with cross-border ambitions ([Alchemist Japan](https://www.alchemistaccelerator.com/japan)).

### 6. High-intent Google Search

Run only after customer interviews identify exact language. Use exact and phrase match around commercial triggers, not broad curiosity. Candidate themes:

- Japan subsidiary setup support
- Japan company launch project manager
- open Japan office foreign company
- Japan entity for first employee
- coordinate Japan company formation bank tax

Exclude terms centered on jobs, tourism, free templates, school assignments, and visa-only demand unless a licensed immigration partner owns that funnel. Send each theme to the same paid readiness offer. Record search term, qualified status, and revenue manually.

### 7. Partner-led practical session

Co-host a 45-minute working session with one licensed professional. Use a real scenario and divide ownership visibly: the professional explains where licensed judgment is required; Hajime explains sequence, dependencies, and preparation. This demonstrates the model without blurring boundaries.

### 8. Reddit and community participation

Use established communities to listen and answer narrowly. Never impersonate a customer, mass-message members, or conceal affiliation. Discussions in Japan-focused finance communities show recurring comparisons among DIY software, professional offices, bank readiness, and English-speaking support, but anecdotes are directional rather than representative ([formation discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/JapanFinance/comments/1mjy8mo/register_kk_via_freee_or_regular_legal_office/), [incorporation cost discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/JapanFinance/comments/171pxg1/setting_up_a_company_costs/)).

The goal is vocabulary and trust, not immediate scale.

### 9. Chambers and trade bodies

Chambers align with cross-border business and can create repeated exposure. The BCCJ explicitly describes strengthening UK-Japan business ties and attracting UK business to Japan ([BCCJ](https://bccjapan.com/japanese)); ACCJ provides a concentrated foreign-business network ([ACCJ](https://www.accj.or.jp/about-j)). Treat these as relationship channels with a longer cycle. Begin with member interviews and useful office hours before buying sponsorship.

## Fastest path to the first 5-20 customers

### Customers 1-5: prove the service

- 2 from professional-partner referrals.
- 2 from founder, investor, or accelerator introductions.
- 1 from trigger-based direct outreach.

Founder target: 30 customer conversations, 10 partner conversations, 75 tailored messages, and three paid readiness engagements within 30 days.

### Customers 6-10: prove one repeatable channel

- Double down on the partner type that produced the best-qualified referrals.
- Run one co-hosted office-hours event each month.
- Publish two practical trigger-specific pages based on actual objections.
- Test USD 500-1,500 in high-intent search.

### Customers 11-20: prove economics

- Require source attribution for every opportunity.
- Track discovery-to-proposal, proposal-to-paid, time-to-close, gross coordination hours, third-party delays, and referral concentration.
- Keep only channels producing customers with acceptable delivery margin and low boundary risk.

## Eight-week validation roadmap

| Week | Action | Decision output |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Recruit interviews; prepare paid readiness offer and qualification rubric | Beachhead list and three price points |
| 2 | Complete first 10 customer and 5 partner interviews | Message and objection revision |
| 3 | Launch referral pilots and 25 trigger-based outreaches | Early channel response |
| 4 | Reach 20 customer and 10 partner interviews; make paid ask | Go/no-go on segment |
| 5 | Deliver first paid readiness engagements manually | Delivery time and perceived value |
| 6 | Run one partner office-hours session and 25 more outreaches | Referral and community signal |
| 7 | Start limited search test only if the offer converted manually | Paid search economics |
| 8 | Review revenue, close rate, source, delivery margin, and objections | Continue, re-segment, or stop |

## Investment gate

Do not authorize material additional product engineering until Hajime has:

- Five paying customers in the beachhead or a clearly superior adjacent segment.
- At least two customers from the same repeatable channel.
- At least one professional partner who makes a second qualified referral.
- A median sales cycle under 45 days.
- Evidence that customers pay for coordination rather than software access.
- A delivery process that stays within the approved professional boundaries.
