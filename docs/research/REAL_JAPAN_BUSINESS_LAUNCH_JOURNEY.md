# The Real Japan Business Launch Journey

## Scope

This map begins when a foreign entrepreneur has made a genuine decision to start operating in Japan. It ends when the business can lawfully perform its intended activity, contract, invoice, receive and make payments, keep compliant records, and employ or pay people as applicable.

It maps the common route to a new Japanese subsidiary, usually a G.K. or K.K., while showing the major branches for a foreign-company branch, a founder who needs Japanese residence status, employees, and regulated activities. It is an operating map, not legal, immigration, tax, accounting, labor, banking, or licensing advice.

There is no universal sequence. Incorporation, office, capital, immigration, banking, permits, tax, and hiring constrain one another. Government, bank, landlord, and professional requirements also vary by case. Waiting times below are practical working ranges, not promises.

## What Official Sources Confirm

- JETRO's March 2026 establishment guidance shows parallel tracks for registration, Foreign Exchange and Foreign Trade Act reporting, tax notices, banking, visas, office, hiring, social insurance, housing, and permits. It estimates roughly two months for a G.K. and two to three months for a subsidiary after the establishment particulars are decided. [JETRO setup overview](https://www.jetro.go.jp/en/invest/setting_up.html) and [JETRO establishment procedures](https://www.jetro.go.jp/invest/setting_up/section1/page3.html).
- Commercial-registration applications made for another party are reserved to attorneys-at-law and judicial scriveners. Japan's licensed professions have separate scopes, so several professionals may be needed. [JETRO professional boundaries](https://www.jetro.go.jp/en/invest/setting_up/section1/reference.html) and [JETRO model commentary](https://www.jetro.go.jp/en/invest/setting_up/guide.html).
- A corporation-establishment notice is generally due to the national tax office within two months. First-year blue-return and executive-compensation elections can have different, consequential deadlines. [National Tax Agency new-corporation guidance](https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/hojin/5100.htm).
- Corporate bank-account requirements vary by bank; screening can be refused without disclosing the reason. Banks commonly request registry and seal certificates, applicant identification, beneficial-owner information, business evidence, and office evidence. [JETRO banking reference](https://www.jetro.go.jp/en/invest/setting_up/section1/reference.html).
- Corporations and covered workers generally enter Employees' Health Insurance and Employees' Pension Insurance; the Japan Pension Service states that covered employee enrollment is due within five days. [Japan Pension Service](https://www.nenkin.go.jp/international/english/japanese-system/employeespension/epi_procedure.html).
- Foreign-investment reporting may be post-transaction or, for designated activities, required before the investment. Classification belongs with qualified counsel; the Bank of Japan publishes the current forms and instructions. [Bank of Japan FEFTA procedures](https://www.boj.or.jp/about/services/tame/index.htm).
- A Corporate Number is designated after incorporation registration, normally without a separate application for a registered corporation. [National Tax Agency Corporate Number system](https://www.houjin-bangou.nta.go.jp/en/setsumei/).

## Hajime Action Vocabulary

- **Explain:** Describe the process, terminology, ownership, and known dependencies without giving regulated advice.
- **Track:** Maintain status, owner, evidence, dependency, and due date.
- **Remind:** Prompt the customer or provider about an agreed action or deadline.
- **Coordinate:** Arrange handoffs, consolidate non-substantive questions, schedule work, and confirm receipt.
- **Escalate:** Surface inactivity, conflicting instructions, missed dates, or an unresolved owner to the customer and responsible professional. Hajime never pressures an agency for a preferred outcome.
- **Stay out:** Do not interpret law, determine eligibility, prepare reserved filings, choose tax treatment, give regulated advice, or predict an institutional decision.

## What the Real Journey Looks Like

The customer does not experience these as 25 completed boxes. They experience several loops:

1. **A decision loop:** office, capital, entity, immigration, tax, and permits repeatedly change one another.
2. **A document loop:** the same fact is requested in different forms, languages, issue dates, certifications, and file formats.
3. **A provider loop:** each professional completes a valid narrow scope, but the customer must detect gaps between scopes.
4. **An institution loop:** banks and agencies request more evidence without committing to approval or a date.
5. **A launch loop:** registration creates a company, but not necessarily a company that can bank, hire, invoice, or perform its intended activity.

## What Is Not a Product

- General explanations of G.K. versus K.K., visas, taxes, banking, or offices are **information only**. Authoritative and professional sources already provide them.
- A generic incorporation checklist is not enough. It cannot determine the correct route, and it becomes dangerous when treated as advice.
- A dashboard is not the product. A dashboard showing stale or ownerless tasks merely displays uncertainty.
- A directory is not the product. A referral without scope definition, handoff ownership, and follow-through leaves the customer coordinating everything.
- Hajime must never sell eligibility judgments, legal interpretation, filing preparation reserved to professionals, tax choices, bank "readiness guarantees," permit conclusions, or outcome predictions.

## Hajime's Real Product

Hajime's real product is **human launch control across professional boundaries**.

It is the reliable answer to five questions:

1. What is the next action?
2. Who owns it?
3. What exact evidence is required?
4. What is blocked, by whom, and for how long?
5. What professional or institutional decision must occur before the customer commits money or proceeds?

The service is valuable only when the customer has multiple interdependent workstreams. For a customer who needs one filing or one professional opinion, Hajime should refer and leave. For a multi-provider launch, Hajime should maintain the approved sequence, document state, owner map, deadlines, handoffs, and escalations while every regulated judgment stays with the responsible professional.

## If Hajime Disappeared Tomorrow

Customers would not most miss articles explaining incorporation. They would not most miss a static checklist or another provider directory.

They would miss:

1. **The current next action:** one unambiguous statement of what must happen now and what cannot start yet.
2. **Cross-provider handoffs:** confirmation that one professional's output reached and was accepted by the next owner.
3. **Document control:** knowing which version, certification, translation, original, and expiration each recipient requires.
4. **Blocker ownership:** knowing whether the customer, a professional, a bank, a landlord, or an agency is holding progress.
5. **Deadline memory:** tax, insurance, immigration, permit, banking, and corporate dates do not wait for the founder to feel ready.
6. **Responsible escalation:** a human notices silence, conflicting instructions, or an unowned dependency and forces clarification without pretending to control the outcome.

That control layer, not information and not software, is the part of the journey most likely to be missed.
