# Translation Variance Audit

## Scope and Method

**Document:** [Immigration Services Agency - Amendment of the landing-criteria ordinances for Business Manager](https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/applications/resources/10_00237.html)

**Audit date:** 2026-07-12  
**Page revision observed:** 2026-06-26  
**Authoritative source:** Japanese rendering (`?lang=ja`)  
**Comparison source:** English rendering displayed by the Ministry page (`?lang=en`)

This is a language-variance audit, not legal advice. The literal-translation column is deliberately close to the Japanese wording. The practical-interpretation column describes what the text communicates to an operational reader; it does not determine eligibility, predict an outcome, or replace advice from a qualified immigration professional.

Confidence describes confidence in the **translation comparison**, not confidence in any applicant's legal position.

## Executive Summary

The English rendering is broadly aligned for dates, numerical thresholds, documentary examples, and most of the 32 detailed Q&A items. The principal risk is not systematic weakening of the requirements. It is localized machine-translation failure in several consequential passages.

Eight differences are high risk:

1. A question about residence-period renewal is mislabeled as a **business license renewal**.
2. The eligible-person limitation for the mandatory full-time employee is internally reversed and malformed in English.
3. The Japanese-language requirement is rendered as an unreadable sentence.
4. The education-or-experience requirement is rendered as an unreadable sentence that obscures the alternative pathways and required field nexus.
5. The permanent-residence and Highly Skilled Professional restriction is truncated before the affected statuses and consequences are stated.
6. The transitional treatment for current Business Manager residents through October 16, 2028 is severely fragmented.
7. The extension of the same treatment to Highly Skilled Professional (i)(c) is truncated.
8. A Q&A question changes **business scale** into **revenue**, even though the Japanese answer concerns invested amounts rather than sales.

The English page should therefore be treated as a navigation aid, not as a safe standalone explanation of employee eligibility, qualification criteria, transition rules, permanent residence effects, Highly Skilled Professional effects, or the 30 million yen evidence test.
