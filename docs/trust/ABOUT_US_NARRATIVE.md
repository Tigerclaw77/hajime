# About Us Narrative

**Status:** Public copy ready for editorial use  
**Primary placement:** About page, proposals, referral introductions

## Short version

Hajime Japan is an independent business formation coordinator for international founders building in Japan. We turn a fragmented process into one clear plan across decisions, licensed professionals, documents, and operational handoffs.

Hajime does not replace attorneys, scriveners, accountants, tax advisers, banks, or government agencies. We make their work easier to navigate and keep the whole launch visible while each responsible party owns their part.

## Full version

Starting a business in Japan is rarely one task. It is a sequence of interdependent decisions involving company formation, capital, office arrangements, professional advice, registrations, banking, tax, insurance, payroll, and the practical work of becoming operational.

Each provider may be capable within their own scope. The founder is still left to understand the sequence, carry information between parties, detect blockers, reconcile timelines, and decide what happens next.

Hajime exists to own that coordination layer.

We begin by making the project legible: the current decision, the workstreams it affects, the responsible person, the expected timeframe, and the evidence needed to move forward. We prepare professional handoffs, consolidate requests, track external waits, and update the plan when reality changes.

Our role is deliberately bounded. Licensed professionals perform regulated legal, tax, accounting, immigration, and filing work. Banks, insurers, landlords, and government bodies make their own decisions. Hajime does not promise their outcomes. We remain accountable for clear planning, careful coordination, accurate status, timely escalation, and honest communication.

That distinction is not a disclaimer added at the end. It is how the service is designed.

## What we believe

- Founders should not have to become process experts to make one important expansion decision.
- Premium service means fewer surprises, not more ceremony.
- A delay should be visible before it becomes a crisis.
- Advice should always be attributable to the qualified person responsible for it.
- Recommendations should solve a customer problem before they create revenue for Hajime.
- Trust grows when a company says what it does not know and what it cannot control.

## One-sentence descriptor

Hajime gives international founders one accountable coordination layer across the people, decisions, and handoffs required to launch a business in Japan.
