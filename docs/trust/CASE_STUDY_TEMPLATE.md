## Evidence checklist

- [ ] Scope and service dates verified
- [ ] Every outcome linked to a record
- [ ] Professional work correctly attributed
- [ ] No confidential or privileged material included
- [ ] Quantitative claim has definition and comparison basis
- [ ] Customer approved title, narrative, quote, attribution, and images
- [ ] Incentive or commercial relationship disclosed
- [ ] Partner names and marks separately approved
- [ ] Final page matches the approved version
- [ ] Reconfirmation date scheduled

## Removal and correction

If a customer withdraws future marketing permission, a fact becomes inaccurate, or the story creates a professional-boundary or privacy concern, unpublish it while review occurs. Preserve the approval and publication record according to the applicable retention rule.
