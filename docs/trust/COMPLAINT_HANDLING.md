# Complaint Handling

**Status:** Public process; verify the contact route and staffing before publication  
**Purpose:** Give customers a route that does not depend on persuading the person whose work is disputed

## Public statement

Customers should be able to question Hajime's work without losing access, slowing their project, or being pressured to stay quiet.

Raise a concern with your coordinator, reply to the project communication thread, or use Hajime's public contact route with ???Complaint??? at the beginning of the message. Do not send sensitive documents through the public form.

## What happens next

1. **Acknowledgment:** Hajime acknowledges the complaint within one business day.
2. **Ownership:** A named person is assigned. When the complaint concerns the coordinator, a senior or independent owner takes over review.
3. **Protection:** Critical deadlines, access, documents, and external work are identified so the complaint does not create avoidable additional harm.
4. **Facts:** Hajime records the customer's concern, requested outcome, relevant commitments, communications, and what remains uncertain.
5. **Review plan:** The customer receives the owner, immediate action, and next review date. The target is a substantive plan within five business days.
6. **Outcome:** Hajime provides the finding, corrective action, commercial remedy where appropriate, and escalation route.
7. **Learning:** Material causes are reviewed without requiring the customer to manage the internal fix.

## What counts as a complaint

A complaint is any expression that Hajime, its representative, or a coordinated partner may have acted unfairly, inaccurately, late, outside scope, insecurely, disrespectfully, or contrary to a stated commitment. The customer does not need to use formal wording.

## Priority complaints

Immediate restricted escalation applies to:

- Suspected data loss or unauthorized access
- Fraud, identity, sanctions, or source-of-funds concern
- Possible unauthorized professional advice or filing activity
- Threatened claim, regulator contact, or professional complaint
- Discrimination, harassment, retaliation, or abusive conduct
- Critical deadline or material customer harm
- Deliberately misleading sales or status representation

## Fairness commitments

- No retaliation for raising a concern
- No deletion or alteration of relevant records
- No speculative admission before facts are established
- No use of confidentiality to suppress lawful, truthful feedback
- No requirement to withdraw a review before receiving a fair remedy
- A partner complaint does not remove Hajime's responsibility to coordinate a response

## Complaint record

Record date, customer, issue, desired outcome, affected deadline, owner, severity, evidence, immediate protection, communications, finding, remedy, customer response, prevention owner, and closure date. Access is restricted to people needed for response and governance.
