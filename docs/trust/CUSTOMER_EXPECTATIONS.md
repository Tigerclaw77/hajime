# Customer Expectations

**Status:** Public and onboarding copy; operations must confirm capacity before publication  
**Purpose:** Replace vague ???concierge??? expectations with mutual, visible responsibilities

## What customers can expect from Hajime

- A named coordinator and a clear backup or escalation route
- A written scope, assumptions, exclusions, fees, and known external costs before payment
- A responsibility map showing what belongs to the customer, Hajime, each partner, and external institutions
- A visible current step, next action, owner, and expected timeframe
- Consolidated requests where practical, rather than avoidable duplicate chasing
- Prepared handoffs to appropriately qualified partners
- Status based on evidence, with ???received,??? ???under review,??? ???submitted,??? and ???accepted??? kept distinct
- Prompt notice when a material assumption, cost, responsibility, or predicted range changes
- Respectful challenge when an action appears inconsistent, incomplete, or outside Hajime's boundary
- A documented complaint and escalation path

## What Hajime needs from customers

- Truthful, complete, and current information
- Clear disclosure of ownership, control, business activity, source of funds, intended operations, and relevant constraints when requested
- Timely review of decisions, documents, and professional advice
- Direct questions to the named professional when regulated judgment is involved
- Authorized signers and required internal approvals
- Payment of agreed Hajime and third-party fees on schedule
- Use of approved communication and document-transfer channels
- Prompt notice of changed facts, availability, priorities, or deadlines
- Respectful communication with Hajime staff and partners
- Acceptance that banks, agencies, professionals, landlords, and insurers retain independent discretion

## Response expectations

The proposal should state the engagement-specific cadence. Unless a written scope says otherwise:

- Hajime acknowledges a material customer message within one business day.
- Acknowledgment may confirm receipt and the next review time rather than provide an immediate substantive answer.
- Urgent means a real deadline, security concern, legal or regulatory exposure, material financial harm, or project-critical failure. It does not mean ordinary preference.
- External waiting does not justify silence; Hajime communicates at the agreed review point even when no decision has arrived.

## If a customer cannot meet a responsibility

Tell Hajime early. The coordinator will identify the impact and, where possible, a lawful support, rescheduling, scope-change, or pause option. Hajime will not silently complete attestations, make decisions, or provide regulated answers on the customer's behalf.

## Poor-fit conditions

Hajime may decline or pause work involving misleading information, concealed ownership or funds, pressure to guarantee an external outcome, abusive conduct, unauthorized professional activity, refusal to engage the responsible professional, or material nonpayment. Necessary protective and handoff steps should be documented.
