# Customer Testimonial Framework

**Status:** Internal collection and publication asset  
**Principle:** A testimonial is customer evidence, not copy disguised as customer speech

## When to ask

Ask only after a meaningful delivered milestone, such as an accepted readiness plan, a completed formation handoff, or a verified launch closeout. Do not ask while a complaint, refund, overdue deliverable, or material escalation is unresolved.

## Neutral request

> Thank you for completing the [milestone] review. We are collecting factual feedback on the experience. There is no obligation to provide a testimonial, and your answer will not affect your service. If you are open to it, would you answer a few questions? We will not publish your words, name, role, or company without showing you the exact proposed use and receiving written approval.

## Interview questions

1. What situation led you to seek help?
2. What did you expect Hajime to coordinate?
3. Which part of the process was most useful?
4. What, if anything, became clearer or easier?
5. Where did the service fall short or create friction?
6. What should a future customer understand before engaging Hajime?
7. Which outcome can you personally verify?
8. Would you describe the service to another founder? In your own words, how?
9. May Hajime propose a short quote for your review?
10. What attribution, if any, would you consider: full name and company, name and role, anonymized descriptor, or private reference only?

Avoid ???How much time did we save???? or ???Would you say Hajime made formation stress-free???? unless the customer independently raised that outcome and can support it.

## Editing rules

- Preserve meaning and tone.
- Correct only grammar, length, or confidential detail with customer approval.
- Do not combine statements from different answers into a stronger claim without showing the exact result.
- Do not add outcome language the customer did not use.
- Do not remove a material qualification.
- Keep the original response and approval record.

## Permission request

> Proposed testimonial: ???[exact final text]???
>
> Proposed attribution: [name, role, company or anonymized description]
>
> Proposed placement: [website page, proposal, social post, or other]
>
> Material disclosure: [none / discounted service / free service / referral relationship / other]
>
> Please confirm that the statement is accurate, that you are authorized to approve the attribution, and that Hajime may use this version in the placements listed. You may decline without affecting your service. Contact us if the statement later becomes inaccurate or you wish to withdraw permission for future use.

## Required record

- Original response and date
- Service milestone and evidence
- Exact approved wording
- Attribution and authority
- Approved placements
- Incentive or commercial relationship
- Approval date and method
- Review or expiry date
- Withdrawal, correction, or complaint history

## Prohibited practices

- Discounts, refunds, gifts, priority, or service benefits conditioned on positive sentiment
- Writing a testimonial and asking the customer to adopt it without a factual interview
- Suppressing negative feedback while representing the sample as typical
- Employee, partner, friend, or investor reviews without clear disclosure
- Reusing a private satisfaction score as public marketing permission
- Naming a company without authorized approval
- Implying one customer's external approval is a typical Hajime outcome

## Evidence basis

The Consumer Affairs Agency states that business-directed reviews and other online representations can be advertising and that advertising not recognizable as such can violate Japan's stealth-marketing rule. See the [CAA guidance](https://www.caa.go.jp/policies/policy/representation/fair_labeling/stealth_marketing/). Counsel should review incentives, disclosures, customer location, and intended placements.
