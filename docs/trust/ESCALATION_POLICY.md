# Escalation Policy

**Status:** Public service standard; verify owners and coverage before publication  
**Purpose:** Explain what Hajime does when normal coordination is no longer enough

## Public statement

Escalation is not a sign that the project has been abandoned. It is the point at which the current owner, plan, or assumption is no longer sufficient and a decision must move to someone with greater authority or different expertise.

Every escalated item receives an owner, immediate protective action where needed, and a next review time.

## Escalation levels

### Level 1: Coordinator resolution

Used for routine clarification, missed response, minor inconsistency, or a task that can be recovered without changing scope or the critical path.

**Customer sees:** Corrected next action and review date.

### Level 2: Senior coordination review

Used when the current owner cannot resolve the issue promptly, a dependency threatens the plan, evidence is rejected, or responsibilities conflict.

**Customer sees:** Impact, remediation owner, options, and updated range.

### Level 3: Operations or partner intervention

Used for repeated service failure, material partner delay, capacity issue, serious scope or fee dispute, replacement decision, or loss of customer confidence.

**Customer sees:** Senior owner, intervention decision, partner or scope options, and any commercial remedy review.

### Level 4: Restricted risk or professional escalation

Used for legal or regulatory exposure, suspected fraud, privacy or security incident, threatened claim, professional-boundary concern, severe reputational risk, partner suspension, or material customer harm.

**Customer sees:** Information appropriate to the circumstances, protection steps, qualified contact, and communication cadence. Some detail may be limited to protect the customer, investigation, legal duties, or other people.

## Mandatory escalation triggers

- A question requires legal, tax, immigration, insurance, financial, filing, or other regulated judgment.
- A material customer deadline is likely to be missed.
- Status cannot be supported by current evidence.
- A partner is no longer available, qualified, independent, or responsive enough for the assignment.
- Scope, fees, or responsibilities are materially disputed.
- Information may have been lost, misdirected, or accessed without authorization.
- Misleading facts, concealed ownership or funds, or unlawful activity are suspected.
- A customer alleges serious harm, retaliation, discrimination, or professional misconduct.

## Customer communication

An escalation update should state:

- What is known and how it is known
- What remains uncertain
- The affected outcome or deadline
- The current owner and escalation owner
- Immediate protection or containment
- Available options and tradeoffs
- What Hajime recommends within its authority
- The next review time

## What Hajime will not do

- Hide an escalation to preserve an optimistic status
- Ask a coordinator to answer outside their competence
- Reinterpret conflicting professional advice as Hajime's conclusion
- Leave a blocked item without an intervention decision date
- Promise an external outcome to calm a customer

## Publication gates

- [ ] Named people cover all four levels
- [ ] Backup and absence coverage exist
- [ ] Partner replacement route exists for every critical assigned category
- [ ] Security and professional-boundary contacts are available
- [ ] Escalation log and customer-update templates are ready
- [ ] The response commitments match actual operating hours
