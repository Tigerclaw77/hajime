# Partner Standards

**Status:** Public standard plus internal publication gate  
**Applies to:** Licensed professionals and material operating providers introduced or coordinated by Hajime

## Public statement

Hajime does not measure a partner network by the number of logos on a page. A partner is useful only when they are qualified for the work, available for the customer, clear about responsibility, careful with information, and willing to communicate when reality changes.

No provider can buy ranking, preferred status, or access to a customer.

## Standards before assignment

### Identity and authority

- Confirm the legal name, business identity, principal contact, and engagement entity.
- For regulated work, verify current registration or authority through an official or profession-controlled source where available.
- Confirm the actual individual responsible for the work, not only the firm brand.

### Capability and fit

- Match the exact question, service, location, language, complexity, customer profile, and deadline.
- Review relevant experience without implying that past work guarantees an outcome.
- Confirm current capacity before presenting a partner as available.

### Independence and conflicts

- Require the professional to perform their own conflict and client-acceptance process.
- Record personal, ownership, referral, or commercial relationships that could affect selection.
- Disclose material referral compensation or economic relationships before customer selection.

### Scope, fees, and responsibility

- Identify the partner's deliverables, exclusions, assumptions, fees, and expected range.
- State whether the customer contracts directly with the partner.
- Keep advice and professional decisions attributable to that partner.

### Communication and service

- Agree a primary contact, backup, update cadence, blocker escalation, and response expectation.
- Require early disclosure of capacity problems, errors, rejected evidence, changed assumptions, or missed commitments.
- Avoid ???in progress??? without a current owner and next review point.

### Information handling

- Share only what is needed for the assigned purpose.
- Confirm the approved transfer method and access list.
- Require prompt reporting of suspected loss, misdirection, or unauthorized access.

## Performance after assignment

Hajime reviews:

- Timeliness against the partner's stated range
- Clarity and attribution of advice or decisions
- Rework caused by avoidable requests or errors
- Communication during uncertainty
- Customer feedback and complaints
- Information-handling incidents
- Scope and fee accuracy
- Willingness to correct mistakes

A partner may be coached, restricted, paused, or removed. Material registration, integrity, independence, quality, privacy, or repeated communication failures require suspension while facts are reviewed.

## Customer rights

- Know who is responsible for the work.
- Receive the partner's scope and fees before commitment.
- Ask how the partner was selected.
- Know about material commercial relationships.
- Decline an introduction or ask for an alternative where reasonably available.
- Raise concerns without retaliation or loss of Hajime complaint access.

## Verification record required

Before saying a partner is ???vetted,??? Hajime must hold a dated record of identity, official registration where applicable, capability, scope, capacity, conflicts, insurance where relevant, data expectations, fees, references or work evidence, and approval owner. Marketing language must say what was checked rather than rely on the undefined word ???vetted.???

## Evidence basis

Official professional sources reinforce the need to verify the responsible professional and role. The [National Tax Agency](https://www.nta.go.jp/english/taxes/consumption_tax/01.htm) describes services reserved to certified public tax accountants; the [Japan Federation of Certified Public Tax Accountants' Associations](https://www.nichizeiren.or.jp/consultation/expart/) provides a current-register search; the Ministry of Justice describes the role of [judicial scriveners](https://www.moj.go.jp/EN/MINJI/shihou.html); and the Immigration Services Agency defines its [application intermediary system](https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/applications/guide/nyuukokukanri07_00262.html). Verification does not transfer professional responsibility to Hajime.
