# Privacy Commitments

**Status:** Public trust copy; counsel must align it with the final privacy notice, operating entity, vendors, data locations, and customer jurisdictions  
**Not a privacy contract:** The final published privacy notice controls legal disclosures.

## Public commitment

Hajime may need sensitive information to coordinate a business launch. That does not create permission to collect everything, keep it forever, or share it broadly.

We make these operating commitments:

### We explain why information is needed

Every material request should identify the purpose, intended recipient or responsible workstream, and whether the information is required by Hajime, a licensed professional, or an external institution.

### We collect the minimum practical amount

Where a status confirmation or document index is enough, Hajime should not retain another full copy. Sensitive information should not be submitted before an approved need and transfer method exist.

### We do not sell customer information

Hajime does not sell personal information or turn customer records into advertising inventory. A partner introduction is made to solve an agreed customer need, not to create hidden access to the customer.

### We limit sharing

Information is shared with a partner or service provider only for an approved purpose and at the level reasonably needed for that task. Commercial relationships do not expand access.

### We keep professional advice attributable

Records should show which professional requested information, provided advice, or accepted evidence. Hajime does not relabel professional material as its own conclusion.

### We respect correction and access requests

Customers may ask what inquiry or project information Hajime holds, request correction of inaccurate information, or request deletion where retention is not required or justified. Hajime verifies the requester and explains the outcome.

### We set retention deliberately

Inquiry records, active project records, completed-project records, complaint records, and legally required business records may need different retention periods. The applicable schedule should be stated or made available, then followed.

### We act on incidents

Suspected loss, unauthorized access, misdirection, or unintended disclosure is not minimized as an inconvenience. Hajime contains the issue, establishes facts, seeks appropriate advice, and notifies affected parties and authorities when required.

## Customer choices

Customers can:

- Ask why a requested item is needed.
- Ask whether confirmation can replace a retained copy.
- Ask who will receive an item.
- Decline optional partner or marketing sharing.
- Request correction, access, or deletion through their coordinator or the public contact route.
- Withdraw testimonial, case-study, or marketing permission for future use, subject to material already lawfully distributed or retained.

## What this does not promise

Hajime will not promise deletion where a record must be preserved for legal, accounting, fraud-prevention, complaint, insurance, or dispute purposes. Hajime will explain the basis and restrict the record appropriately.

## Evidence basis

The [Personal Information Protection Commission's APPI materials](https://www.ppc.go.jp/en/legal/) provide the authoritative Japanese privacy framework. PPC guidance on third-party platforms says businesses should understand information flows, avoid unintended collection, and clearly disclose collection through privacy notices. These commitments are deliberately narrower than a legal compliance claim and require counsel review against Hajime's actual operations.
