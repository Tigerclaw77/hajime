# Professional Boundaries

**Status:** Public copy; mandatory Japanese counsel review before use  
**Purpose:** Make it impossible for a reasonable customer to mistake Hajime for the regulated professional responsible for advice or filings

## Plain-language statement

Hajime is a business formation coordinator. We organize the plan, prepare handoffs, consolidate requests, track responsibilities and evidence, communicate status, and escalate blockers.

Hajime is not a law firm, accounting or tax firm, immigration representative, bank, insurer, real-estate broker, government agency, notary, translator, or filing professional.

When work requires regulated judgment, advice, representation, certification, solicitation, or filing authority, the appropriately qualified professional performs that work and remains responsible for it.

## Responsibility table

| Need | Hajime may do | Responsible party owns |
|---|---|---|
| Company formation | Plan sequence, prepare facts, coordinate signatures and handoff, track status | Legal conclusions, registration documents, filing, professional acceptance |
| Immigration | Gather operational context, prepare questions, schedule and track | Eligibility advice, application strategy, representation, authorized submission |
| Tax and accounting | Prepare intake, track requested records and dates, coordinate setup | Tax advice, elections, tax documents, representation, accounting opinions |
| Banking | Prepare business context and evidence index, coordinate introduction and follow-up | Due diligence, suitability, account decision, financial services |
| Insurance | Identify operational need, introduce licensed provider, coordinate activation status | Product recommendation, solicitation, suitability, binding coverage |
| Office or property | Clarify needs, compare factual provider terms, schedule communication | Brokerage, negotiation, legal address suitability, lease advice |
| Translation and certification | Control versions and coordinate provider delivery | Translation accuracy, notarization, apostille, certification, recipient acceptance |
| Government review | Record submission evidence, external range, request, and next check | Decision, interpretation, approval, rejection, processing time |

## How advice is communicated

- The source professional is named.
- Assumptions and limitations remain attached.
- Hajime may convert an approved conclusion into operational tasks but does not alter its meaning.
- When professionals disagree, Hajime coordinates clarification or an independent qualified opinion; it does not choose the legal or tax answer.

## Questions Hajime must escalate

Hajime pauses and routes questions about legal structure, immigration eligibility, tax treatment, filing sufficiency, insurance suitability, regulated licenses, legal response to rejection or dispute, and any strategy intended to influence an approval through characterization of ownership, funds, activity, or evidence.

## Outcomes Hajime never guarantees

- Incorporation or registration acceptance
- Visa or residence status
- Bank account, credit, or payment access
- Tax treatment
- Insurance availability or suitability
- Office or address acceptance
- License or permit
- Government or partner timing

Hajime may commit only to actions it controls, such as preparing a handoff by an agreed date or updating a customer after a scheduled review.

## Evidence basis

The conservative boundary reflects Japan's [Attorneys Act, including Article 72](https://www.japaneselawtranslation.go.jp/en/laws/view/4610/tb), the [National Tax Agency description of reserved tax services](https://www.nta.go.jp/english/taxes/consumption_tax/01.htm), the Ministry of Justice's [judicial scrivener overview](https://www.moj.go.jp/EN/MINJI/shihou.html), and the Immigration Services Agency's [application intermediary system](https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/applications/guide/nyuukokukanri07_00262.html). These references do not define Hajime's complete perimeter. Counsel must review actual conduct, scripts, economics, and engagements.
