# Public Roadmap

**Status:** Public copy  
**Last reviewed:** July 12, 2026  
**Review cadence:** Monthly during validation and whenever the supported service changes

## How to read this roadmap

This page distinguishes what Hajime offers today from what is being validated and what may come later. It is not a promise of release dates. Services move forward only when customer evidence, qualified partners, operating capacity, security, economics, and professional boundaries support them.

## Available today

### Japan launch readiness

Hajime can help a founder clarify business context, open decisions, likely provider needs, dependencies, responsibilities, and a sequenced launch plan.

### Business formation coordination

For an appropriately scoped Japan project, Hajime can coordinate the formation plan, professional handoffs, document requests, capital and office dependencies, filing status, and post-formation transition. Licensed professionals perform regulated work.

### Professional and provider coordination

Depending on verified fit and availability, Hajime can coordinate introductions and handoffs across:

- Judicial scriveners and administrative scriveners
- Attorneys and immigration professionals
- Accountants and certified public tax accountants
- Virtual office and coworking providers
- Banking introductions and readiness
- Insurance, payroll, and HR providers

Availability is not guaranteed, and each provider retains their own acceptance, scope, fees, and professional responsibility.

### Founder-led service

Hajime is in early commercial validation. Delivery is deliberately founder-led and manually coordinated. That is a maturity fact, not a claim of scale.

## Being validated now

- Whether customers will pay a premium for independent coordination
- Which package scope creates the clearest value
- Realistic effort, duration, and external-cost ranges
- Partner fit, responsiveness, and repeat performance
- Customer comprehension of professional boundaries
- Complaint, refund, privacy, and security operations through real engagements

Validation means these elements may change. Any active customer receives the scope and price they accepted unless a change is agreed in writing.

## Planned only after evidence

### Post-launch operating coordination

Ongoing accounting, tax, payroll, insurance, banking, and obligation coordination may be offered after repeated customer demand and viable manual delivery.

### Broader partner coverage

Additional capacity and regions may be added after partner standards are demonstrated through real work. Hajime will not publish a large directory as a substitute for quality.

### Housing and relocation support

Housing remains a future adjacent service. It requires clear customer demand, licensed delivery paths, privacy controls, and a scope that does not distract from business launch coordination.

### Additional countries

Hajime does not currently offer general multi-country expansion. Any future country requires its own professional-boundary review, partner coverage, service manual, economics, and limited pilot.

## Not offered

- Legal, tax, immigration, banking, insurance, or investment advice from Hajime
- Guaranteed registration, visa, bank account, license, or completion date
- A paid partner-ranking marketplace
- Customer data sold for advertising
- AI-generated professional advice or customer chatbot
- Unsupported ???all-inclusive??? formation

## Evidence policy

Future items will not move into ???available today??? because they sound useful or make Hajime appear larger. The change requires real customer demand, a lawful and bounded delivery model, qualified capacity, operational readiness, and a public statement that matches actual service.
