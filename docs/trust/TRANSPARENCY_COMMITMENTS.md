# Transparency Commitments

**Status:** Public copy; founder and operations sign-off required  
**Purpose:** Define what Hajime will reveal even when disclosure is commercially uncomfortable

## Public commitment

Hajime's value depends on being a trusted coordinator. We will not protect that image by hiding uncertainty, commercial relationships, delays, mistakes, or our early operating stage.

## What we disclose

### Scope and cost

- What Hajime will and will not do
- What is included in the package
- Known assumptions and change triggers
- Hajime fees, government charges, professional fees, and other external costs as separate categories
- Whether a price is established, experimental, fixed, milestone-based, or scoped by proposal

### Responsibility

- The named Hajime coordinator
- The professional or provider responsible for regulated work
- Whether the customer contracts directly with that party
- Who owns the current action, decision, and timing estimate

### Partner economics and conflicts

- Material referral fees, commissions, ownership, volume arrangements, or other economic relationships that could reasonably affect trust
- Personal or business conflicts relevant to the recommendation
- The customer's ability to decline or request another suitable option

No provider can pay for ranking, urgency, or customer-data access.

### Status and timing

- The evidence behind ???received,??? ???under review,??? ???submitted,??? ???accepted,??? and ???complete???
- Whether a date is a Hajime commitment, partner estimate, external range, or target
- Material changed assumptions and forecast impact
- When nothing changed and the next review point

### Maturity and evidence

- That Hajime is in early commercial validation
- Only real, permissioned customer stories and reviews
- No invented customer count, partner network size, approval rate, savings, launch-speed, or satisfaction metric
- Clear labeling of future services and unvalidated offers

### Errors and corrections

When Hajime discovers a material error in customer or public information, it should correct the statement, identify affected customers, explain the impact, and record the prevention action. Corrections should not be hidden solely because the original statement was favorable.

## What transparency does not require

Hajime will not publish customer confidential information, another person's private data, privileged professional communication, security details that increase risk, or investigation material whose disclosure would create harm or violate duties. The reason for limiting detail should be explained where possible.

## Trust test

Before withholding a material fact, ask:

> Would a reasonable customer make a different decision about buying, continuing, sharing information, choosing a partner, or acting on the project if they knew this fact?

If yes, the default is prompt, accurate disclosure unless a legal, safety, security, or confidentiality duty requires a narrower communication.

## Evidence basis

The Consumer Affairs Agency explains that false or exaggerated representations can undermine informed choice and that undisclosed business-directed reviews or posts can constitute prohibited stealth marketing. See the [CAA stealth-marketing guidance](https://www.caa.go.jp/policies/policy/representation/fair_labeling/stealth_marketing/) and [consumer transaction overview](https://www.caa.go.jp/about_us/about/caa_pamphlet/jp_2026_011.html). Counsel should review how these rules apply to Hajime's buyers and marketing.
