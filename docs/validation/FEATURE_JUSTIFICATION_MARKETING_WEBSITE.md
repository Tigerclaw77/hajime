# Feature Justification: Public Marketing Website

## Problem being solved

Hajime has no production public path that explains the service, establishes operating boundaries, presents pricing, and converts a qualified visitor into a lead. Founder-led outreach currently has nowhere credible to send a prospect and requires the founder to repeat basic qualification and trust-building information manually.

## Who benefits

- Prospective founders evaluating business formation in Japan
- The founder, who receives structured inquiries instead of unqualified messages
- Sales operations, because website inquiries enter the existing Lead domain as `Website / New`

## Assumption validated

Qualified international founders will book a discovery call when Hajime presents a clear coordination offer, transparent package structure, credible professional boundaries, and a low-friction inquiry path.

## Expected ROI

The website creates a reusable destination for every active outreach and referral experiment. One converted Guided Launch engagement should repay the implementation cost. It also reduces repeated explanation and manual lead entry on every inquiry.

## Estimated development time

Two to three focused engineering days for the public pages, lead submission path, metadata, tests, and responsive/accessibility QA.

## Why now instead of later

Customer acquisition is the active constraint. The internal platform is already sufficient for validation, but outbound messages, referrals, and interviews cannot compound without a trustworthy conversion destination. This work directly supports the current validation plan and does not expand service operations.

## Success metrics

- At least 8% of qualified visitors begin the consultation form
- At least 3% of qualified visitors submit a consultation request
- At least 70% of submitted website leads meet the discovery qualification threshold
- Fewer than 10% of prospects require clarification about Hajime's role before booking
- Every valid submission appears as a `New` lead with source `Website`

## Kill criteria

- Fewer than five qualified visits reach the consultation page after 100 targeted visits
- Zero valid submissions after 200 targeted visits and two messaging tests
- Most interviewees still misunderstand Hajime as a law firm or regulated provider
- The form creates material spam or operational burden that exceeds qualified lead value

If a kill criterion is reached, stop feature work. Return to direct manual outreach and interview evidence before changing the website.
