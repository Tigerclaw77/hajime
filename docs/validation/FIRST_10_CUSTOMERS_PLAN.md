# Hajime Japan First 10 Customers Plan

## Purpose

Prove that commercially committed founders will pay Hajime for launch coordination before further product investment.

A customer counts only when all three events occur:

1. A written scope is accepted.
2. The customer pays a non-refundable readiness fee or the required project deposit.
3. Hajime schedules delivery.

Interviews, free pilots, letters of intent, proposal requests, and verbal commitments do not count as customers.

## Target customer

Focus on English-speaking, founder-led B2B technology or professional-services companies with:

- 2-50 employees.
- Existing revenue or institutional funding.
- A concrete Japan trigger within 12 months.
- At least three interdependent launch needs.
- No internal Japan operations lead.
- Budget for Hajime in addition to professional and government fees.

Valid triggers include an imminent Japanese customer, partner, hire, founder relocation, or board-approved market-entry date.

## Validation offer

Lead with one paid entry product: **Japan Launch Readiness**.

Deliver within ten business days after complete intake:

- Current-state and readiness summary.
- Decision and dependency map.
- Responsibility map separating customer, Hajime, professional, and institution ownership.
- Preliminary timeline and cost ranges with assumptions.
- Prioritized next actions.
- One 60-minute review session.
- Recommendation to stop, self-manage, use a specialist directly, or continue with Hajime.

The initial test price is USD 1,500. Professional advice and third-party fees are excluded. Different price tests are governed by `PRICING_EXPERIMENTS.md` and should not be improvised during a call.

## Customer #1: prove a stranger will pay

### Objective

Close and deliver one paid readiness engagement to a qualified customer. Learn whether the customer values independent coordination enough to pay before software, testimonials, or a mature partner network exists.

### Channels

1. Warm introductions from the founder's personal and professional network.
2. Direct introductions from existing accountants, attorneys, founders, investors, or Japan-connected operators.
3. Ten highly tailored messages to companies with a visible Japan trigger.

Do not use paid advertising for Customer 1.

### Expected conversion

| Funnel stage | Target |
|---|---:|
| Warm introduction requests | 20 |
| Introductions or positive replies | 8-10 |
| Qualified discovery calls | 4-6 |
| Written offers | 2-3 |
| Paying customers | 1 |

Expected qualified discovery-to-paid conversion: 17-25%. This is a planning assumption, not a benchmark.

### Founder activities

- Build a named list of 40 potential connectors and prospects.
- Request introductions individually, explaining the exact trigger and customer profile.
- Hold discovery calls personally.
- Listen for the customer's current process, cost of delay, internal coordination burden, decision authority, and actual budget.
- Make a concrete paid offer to every qualified prospect within one business day.
- Deliver the work manually and record every hour, repeated request, scope surprise, and customer question.
- Ask what nearly prevented the purchase and what outcome justified the fee.

### Weekly targets

| Week | Activity target | Outcome target |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 20 introduction requests, 10 direct messages, 5 partner conversations | 8 positive replies and 4 calls booked |
| 2 | 4-6 qualified discoveries, 2-3 same-day offers, daily follow-up | 1 signed scope and payment |
| 3 | Deliver readiness engagement and review session | On-time delivery, customer value rating of at least 8/10 |

### Decision criteria

**Advance to Customers 2-5 when:** one qualified buyer pays at least USD 750, completes intake, and says the output changed a decision, sequence, provider choice, or workload.

**Repeat Customer 1 stage when:** qualified prospects request proposals but timing or trust prevents payment. Improve proof, scope clarity, or follow-up for two more weeks.

**Revisit the offer when:** six qualified discoveries produce no payment, or customers only want regulated professional advice.

## Customers #2-5: prove a repeatable problem and delivery method

### Objectives

- Close four additional paying customers.
- Confirm that the same core coordination problem appears across customers.
- Prove at least two customers can come from the same channel.
- Establish the actual delivery hours and gross margin of the readiness offer.
- Obtain two permissioned references or anonymized case summaries.

### Channels

Allocate founder acquisition time as follows:

- 40% professional-partner referrals.
- 30% founder, investor, and accelerator introductions.
- 20% trigger-based direct outreach.
- 10% useful participation in relevant founder communities.

Run four 60-day referral pilots: one bilingual accountant, one judicial scrivener, one administrative scrivener or immigration professional, and one international business attorney. No referral commissions during validation.

### Expected conversion

| Funnel stage | Target for four customers |
|---|---:|
| Tailored prospects or introductions | 80-100 |
| Positive replies | 20-25 |
| Qualified discovery calls | 12-16 |
| Written offers | 8-10 |
| Paying customers | 4 |

Expected qualified discovery-to-paid conversion: 25-33%. Expected proposal acceptance: 40-50%.

### Founder activities

- Meet 10 potential referral partners and activate four pilots.
- Send 20-25 tailored outreach or introduction requests each week.
- Hold three or four qualified discoveries each week.
- Send proposals within one business day using the same core scope.
- Conduct a 15-minute lost-deal interview whenever permission is given.
- Deliver all work manually from standard templates.
- Review actual effort, cycle time, and scope variance after each customer.
- Ask every satisfied customer for one specific introduction, not a general referral request.
- Publish no broad claims; use only permissioned, factual outcomes.

### Weekly targets

| Metric | Weekly target |
|---|---:|
| New named prospects | 20 |
| Tailored outreach or introduction requests | 20-25 |
| Partner meetings | 2-3 until four pilots are active |
| Qualified discoveries | 3-4 |
| Proposals | 2-3 |
| New paying customers | 0.5-1 average |
| Lost-deal interviews | 1 |
| Referral asks after delivery | 1 per satisfied customer |

Timebox: four to six weeks after Customer 1.

### Decision criteria

**Advance to Customers 6-10 when all are true:**

- Five total customers have paid.
- At least three match the target segment.
- At least two came from the same channel.
- Median discovery-to-payment time is 45 days or less.
- At least three rate the service 8/10 or higher.
- Median delivery effort supports a positive contribution margin at the tested price.
- Customers describe the value as coordination, clarity, preparation, or saved time rather than access to software.

**Pivot the segment or offer when any are true:**

- More than half of paying demand comes from a different coherent segment.
- Most buyers purchase only at USD 750 and reject higher prices.
- The deliverable is valued, but customers consistently want a narrower paid first step.
- Professional-only inquiries dominate qualified conversations.

**Stop the current approach when:** 20 qualified discoveries and at least 10 written offers produce fewer than two paying customers.

## Customers #6-10: prove channel and economic repeatability

### Objectives

- Acquire five more paying customers without materially increasing founder hours per sale.
- Produce at least three customers from one repeatable channel.
- Validate a stable price and package boundary.
- Demonstrate repeat referrals from at least one partner.
- Decide whether the business merits continued founder investment.

### Channels

1. Double down on the best-performing referral partner category.
2. Run one partner-led Japan readiness clinic each month.
3. Continue trigger-based outreach only for signals that converted in Customers 1-5.
4. Test a narrowly targeted landing page and up to USD 1,000 of high-intent search only after manual channels have converted.
5. Ask prior customers for introductions at a defined success moment.

Do not add broad social advertising, automated outreach, affiliate marketplaces, or a larger partner directory.

### Expected conversion

| Funnel stage | Target for five customers |
|---|---:|
| Qualified introductions or targeted positive replies | 25-35 |
| Qualified discovery calls | 15-20 |
| Written offers | 10-14 |
| Paying customers | 5 |

Expected qualified discovery-to-paid conversion: 25-33%. Expected proposal acceptance: 40% or better. At least three customers should originate from referrals.

### Founder activities

- Spend 60% of acquisition time on the strongest proven channel.
- Hold a 30-minute weekly pipeline and experiment review.
- Ask active partners what made each referred customer ready or unready.
- Track founder sales hours, acquisition cost, delivery hours, and gross contribution by source.
- Use the same offer and price for a cohort of at least five comparable prospects before changing either.
- Run one price or packaging variable at a time.
- Ask for a referral and satisfaction rating at the readiness review and again after a meaningful launch milestone.

### Weekly targets

| Metric | Weekly target |
|---|---:|
| Qualified partner referrals | 2 |
| Trigger-qualified direct prospects | 10-15 |
| Qualified discoveries | 3-4 |
| Proposals | 2-3 |
| New paying customers | 0.75-1 average |
| Partner follow-ups | 4 |
| Customer referral asks | 2 |
| Experiment review | 1 |

Timebox: five to eight weeks after Customer 5.

### Decision criteria

**Continue the business when all are true by Customer 10:**

- Ten total customers have paid.
- At least six match one coherent segment.
- At least three came from one repeatable channel.
- At least one partner made a second qualified referral.
- Proposal acceptance is at least 35%.
- Median time to close is 45 days or less.
- Median readiness selling price is at least USD 1,500 or larger-project deposits demonstrate stronger economics.
- At least 70% of completed customers rate value 8/10 or higher.
- Delivery is possible without software and without crossing professional boundaries.

**Pivot when:** payment exists but the segment, offer, channel, or price misses one or more thresholds for two consecutive four-week reviews.

**Stop when:** fewer than five of 30 qualified prospects pay, no channel produces a second customer, or delivery cannot achieve a positive contribution margin without under-scoping the work.

## Operating cadence

Every Friday, update one scorecard with:

- New prospects by source.
- Qualified discoveries.
- Proposals and stated objections.
- Signed and paid customers.
- Revenue and selling price.
- Days to close.
- Founder acquisition hours.
- Delivery hours and direct costs.
- Customer value rating.
- Referrals requested and received.

Do not change the segment, message, channel, and price simultaneously. Select one uncertainty, run a timeboxed test, and record the result.
